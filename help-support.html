Frequently asked questions

When does an allocation release?

Releases follow the schedule selected for the plan. The production scheduler will process eligible releases server-side.

When can I use emergency access?

For eligible locked plans, emergency access becomes available after half of the original lock duration and can be used once under the plan rules.

What happens at maturity?

The remaining eligible plan balance returns to the available wallet when the plan reaches maturity.

Why does KYC show pending?

Identity verification requires review. The interface is prepared for a secure KYC provider/backend connection.
